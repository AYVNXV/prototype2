Who We Are

Building Trust Since 2016

LuxeEstates was founded in 2016 with a singular vision: to redefine the real estate experience in Pakistan. We set out to build a firm where every client — whether buying their first home or expanding a multi-property portfolio — is treated with the utmost respect, transparency, and dedication.

Headquartered in DHA Lahore, we have grown into one of Pakistan's most recognized luxury real estate brands, operating across Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, and Rawalpindi. Our team of seasoned consultants, legal experts, and marketing professionals work in harmony to deliver seamless property transactions from the very first inquiry to the final key handover.

With over 500 satisfied clients, 20+ premium listings, and a reputation built on integrity, LuxeEstates continues to lead Pakistan's premium real estate sector. We believe that finding the right property is not just a transaction — it is the beginning of a new chapter, and we are honoured to be part of yours.
